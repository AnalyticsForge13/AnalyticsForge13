# 📌 Featured Analytics Projects

## 🚚 Supply Chain Late-Delivery Risk Analysis

**Python • Pandas • Scikit-learn**

Analyzed **180K+ order records** to identify patterns behind late deliveries and quantify their business impact.

### 🔍 Key Findings

* **54.7%** of orders were shipped late
* Late deliveries were concentrated in specific regions and shipping modes
* Quantified approximately **$2.1M in profit loss**
* Profit impact represented **28% of $7.5M total profit**
* Built a Random Forest classifier to identify at-risk orders
* Applied **SMOTE class balancing**
* Achieved **77% precision** in flagging at-risk orders

`Data Cleaning` `EDA` `Feature Engineering` `Machine Learning` `Classification` `Business Analysis`

## 👥 Customer Churn Analysis

**Python • SQL • Power BI**

Analyzed **7,000+ customer records** to identify churn patterns, customer segments, and potential drivers of customer attrition.

### 🔍 Key Findings

* Overall churn rate: **26.5%**
* Month-to-month contract churn: **42.7%**
* Two-year contract churn: **2.8%**
* Average monthly charge among analyzed groups:

  * Higher-charge customers: **$74.42**
  * Comparison average: **$61.27**
* Identified pricing pressure as an important churn-related factor

### 📊 Deliverable

Built an interactive **Power BI dashboard** using insights generated from Python and SQL analysis.
